# Freeze plan and tracker: 5 Dec 2026

**This is the one live tracker.** It replaces the older plan files listed in section 5. Written 2026-11-27, after PRs #84, #83 and #85 were merged (`main` at `2fb9ea3`).

**Goal of the freeze:** a recorded portfolio demo that stands out. Not a pilot. Not public hosting.

## 1. Rules

- One step at a time, in the order below.
- Decision, ADR, spec, tests, code. Stop for the owner's go after the ADR and spec.
- Commits stay local until the owner says to push. The owner merges with "Create a merge commit".
- After a merge: `git pull github main`, then `git push origin main`.
- Answer material never goes into this repo. A paid run needs a printed estimate, and the owner runs it.
- When a step ends, update its Status here in the same PR.
