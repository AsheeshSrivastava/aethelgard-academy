## 7. What blocks your own testing first, and a recommended order

1. **No content after Stage 0.** You can walk Stage 0 and PY-0.2 only. Everything else, Make steps included, needs lessons and seeds. This is the main blocker, and it is writing, not code.
2. **Make has no screen yet.** Before writing Stage 1, add the small app pieces so a Make step is visible and testable: show `Unit.make.task` on the Build step, mark Build seeds that are Make steps and give them a blank starter, and add the checks (Make seed present, starter blank). Then write PY-1.2's Make step first as the pattern.
3. **Test-mode entries count in your progress.** Decide now whether your stress-test work should count. If not, exclude tagged entries from the estimator (one switch) before you test further.
4. **Multi-file builds.** Needed by Python Stage 5 builds and every later stage, and by the 30 Own targets. Build it before writing the Stage 5 build.
5. **Curriculum guards** (cheap, protect the content you are about to write): entry and one-rung check (A1.1, A1.2), awareness topics never in Cold Check (A1.4), volume warning (A1.6).
6. **Stage builds and maturity** (ADR-0018): add `maturity` and `kind` when the second build is written; write builds stage by stage with the content.
7. **Before anyone else learns:** move answer keys out of the public repo, Docker sandbox on by default, consent, deletion and retention (ADR-0022), close the AXIS citation exception, and accept or revise the Proposed ADRs.
